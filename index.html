Two-compartment forward model · Eq. 1
dCₚ/dt = FₚCₐ − (Fₚ + Fₜ)Cₚ/vₚ
dCₜ/dt = FₜCₚ/vₚ − Cₜ/Tₜ

The vascular and tubular states are tracer amounts per unit tissue volume; total tissue concentration is Cₚ + Cₜ. Flow rates have units s⁻¹ and transit time is in seconds.

Background calibration · invariant effective products
aₖ = Q₀.₂(φₖ)
F⁰′ = F⁰(1 − a),   φ′ = (φ − a)/(1 − a)

The expression applies to flow and filtration. For transit, T⁰′ = T⁰(1 + 2a₃) and φ₃′ = (φ₃ − a₃)/(1 + 2a₃). Clipping can introduce small forward discrepancies.

OBSERVATION RESOLUTION

Spatial detail is not spatial identifiability.

At the specified homogeneous reference, J = W ⊗ K. Three parameters at 512 points give 1,536 unknowns; 32 regions bound the local rank by 96. Evaluated heterogeneous states have numerical ranks of 210 and 237.

These are local diagnostics at specified states and tolerances. Image pixels, simulated scenarios and numerical ranks do not replace independent clinical samples or a proof of global identifiability.

How should the uncertainty results be interpreted?

The original regular-stride subset contains only normal and flow abnormalities; filtration and transit truth are zero. Later mechanism-balanced checks use 120 validation scenarios and 180 test scenarios. Validation-calibrated ensemble coverage is 89.7%, 90.0% and 89.9% for flow, filtration and transit, with widths of 0.053, 0.053 and 0.023.

These intervals describe the specified simulation domain and original anatomy partition. Post-correction coverage and new-patient coverage have not been validated. Reference Gaussian intervals remain local-resolution diagnostics; their interval scores did not outperform the calibrated ensemble.

    RESEARCH SUMMARY

    DigitalKidney presents an anatomy-conditioned renal physiological inversion framework. An established vascular–tubular model generates controlled dynamic MR observations. Quality-guided normal-reference initialization and prior anchoring are evaluated with fixed-budget refinement. Acquired images and isotope split function provide distinct component checks. Simulated physiological labels, reused test anatomy, local identifiability diagnostics and unestablished clinical spatial accuracy are reported as separate evidence levels.
